BUD is a free app by CMDLAB (cmdlabtech) for Even Realities G2 glasses. By using it you agree to these terms.

## What BUD does
BUD turns what you say into text with xAI's speech-to-text, and sends that text to your own Grok Bot through
BUD's forwarder. Your bots do the work and reply in the Grok Bot app. Approvals always stay in the Grok Bot app;
nothing you say through BUD approves anything.

## Your accounts and costs
You need your own Grok Bot account and your own xAI API key. xAI bills you for speech-to-text under your xAI
account. You are responsible for your accounts, your keys, and what your bots do with your requests. Keep your
keys private; BUD stores them only on your phone.
